# SWEAT440 Standing Creative & Brand Standards

## Brand Standards (Non-Negotiable)

### Brand Name
- Always: SWEAT440 — all caps, no spaces, no hyphens, single consistent color
- "SWEAT" and "440" must never appear in different colors from one another
- The shield logo with "SWEAT" and "440" separated around it is APPROVED brand usage
- The shield logo alone (without the full brand name) is NOT sufficient
- "SWEAT440" must appear in full on every creative asset — static and video
- Never write "Sweat440," "sweat440," "SWEAT 440," or any other variant

### Workout Descriptor
- Always: "strength-based" or "circuit training"
- Never: "HIIT" — this is a deliberate brand positioning decision
- Never: cardio-forward visual or copy framing

### Colors (digital)
- Primary: #00A3E0 (main brand blue)
- Supporting: #99DAF3 (light blue), #0084B5 (deep blue), #D2EFFF (pale blue)
- Accent: #00FFC2 (digital only), #DDFF00 (CTAs, callouts, lime/yellow)
- No colors outside this palette

### Typography
- Headers: Oswald — ALL CAPS and Italics
- Body: Open Sans
- No other fonts

## Copy Standards (Non-Negotiable)

### Language to Always Avoid
- "HIIT" — brand positioning violation
- "Zero excuses" / "no excuses" / "no excuse left" — implies reader has been making
  excuses; creates friction for every persona
- "Get" — avoid in all writing (brand preference)
- Em dashes — avoid in all writing (brand preference)
- All-caps body copy — aggressive tone, undermines warmth for most personas

### All-Caps Guidance
- Acceptable: DINKs and Parents with Older Children (authority and premium tone)
- Not acceptable: Stay-at-Home Parents, Parents with Young Children, Single Individuals
- Default recommendation: sentence case across all personas

### CTA Standards
- Command verbs: "Book," "Claim," "Reserve," "Start," "Tap," "Show up"
- Must tell the reader what happens next: "Tap 'Book Now' to claim your first class"
- "Claim" implies ownership and entitlement — strong for most personas
- "Just show up" — strongest P1 and P3 action phrase
- "See it for yourself" / "See what a real system delivers" — strong P5 closes
- "Book now" is the weakest action phrase — always flag and replace
- Urgency is only permitted when a real deadline or capacity limit exists — never manufactured

### Friction Language — Remove From All CTAs
The following must NEVER appear in CTA copy:
- "First-time visitors only" — moves to form fine print if legally required
- "Local ID required" — moves to form fine print if legally required
- Any restriction qualifier at the conversion moment

Standing team instruction to include in every review where this appears:
"Please remove 'first-time visitors only' and 'local ID required' from all CTA copy
across every placement. If disclosure is legally required, place it in the form fine
print below the submit button — never in the CTA itself."

### Urgency Rules
- Only use urgency when a genuine deadline, limited enrollment block, or seasonal
  offer exists
- Never manufacture scarcity
- False urgency damages trust and may violate Meta's advertising policies

## Meta Platform Standards

### Relevance Diagnostics (Current)
Meta retired the single "Relevance Score" in 2019. The current system uses three
separate rankings — reference these, never "Relevance Score":
- Quality Ranking
- Engagement Rate Ranking
- Conversion Rate Ranking

### Character Limits by Placement
| Placement | Practical Limit |
|-----------|----------------|
| Feed primary text (mobile) | ~125 characters before "See More" |
| Headline | 40 characters (truncates in some placements) |
| Stories / Reels caption | Under 90 characters or use on-screen text |
| Carousel card headline | 40 characters per card |

### Hook Types by Placement
| Placement | Best Hook Types |
|-----------|----------------|
| Google Search | Question + Conditional |
| Meta Feed cold | Confession + Reframe |
| Meta Reels cold | Relief + Confession |
| Meta retargeting | Conditional + Relief |
| UGC caption | Confession → Reframe → Relief |

### Testing Standards
- Minimum two copy variants per creative asset per campaign launch
- Test one variable at a time: hook type, CTA phrasing, copy length, social proof,
  tone
- Minimum test window: 7 days or 1,000 impressions per variant
- Declare winner on cost-per-lead, not CTR alone
- Creative fatigue on Meta can set in within days for smaller geo audiences
- Refresh copy every 2–3 weeks minimum

### Social Proof (Required Element)
Every campaign must include at least one social proof element in rotation:
- Member testimonial snippets
- Star ratings / review counts ("4.9 stars across our locations")
- Result-based callouts
- Attendance or community facts
Place after the hook, before the CTA — as a trust bridge

## Recurring Flags (Check Every Review)

These issues appeared repeatedly across the campaign reviewed in this session.
Check for all of them on every review without being asked:

1. **Full brand name missing from creative asset** — shield alone is not sufficient
2. **"First-time visitors only" or "local ID required" in CTA** — always flag and instruct removal
3. **All-caps copy** — flag for inappropriate personas, recommend sentence case
4. **"Book now" as CTA** — weakest action phrase, always provide alternative
5. **10-minute differentiator missing from creative** — flag if 40-minute duration leads instead
6. **#NEVERLATE without anchor copy** — needs "A new class starts every 10 minutes" to function
7. **P5 language in cold non-P5 campaign** — flag as premature results messaging
8. **"Zero/no excuses" language** — friction for every persona, always replace
9. **Subject too athletic for P1 campaign** — flag if subject reinforces appearance anxiety
10. **Two variants too similar** — flag if Meta has no meaningful creative variable to learn from
11. **Comma splices in headlines** — flag and provide clean alternative
12. **Disclosure language inside action line** — always move to form fine print
13. **"No plan? No problem" in non-P2 campaign** — Decision Relief language, flag if mismatched
14. **Emoji mid-paragraph** — breaks tone, flag if used outside offer line or bullets
