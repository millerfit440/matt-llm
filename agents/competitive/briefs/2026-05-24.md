### OrangeTheory Fitness

OrangeTheory's most significant move this cycle: registration is open now (through June 14) for its Season of Strength challenge, running June 1 – July 26. Entry is $35 and includes two InBody body composition scans and eight weeks of strength-tracking inside class. The challenge explicitly reframes OTF away from its signature "splat points" (heart rate zones) and toward foundational strength benchmarks — participants track one of four lifts (chest press, single-arm row, squat, deadlift) per class. TRUBAR is the official title sponsor. Separately, OTF is running a May-only acquisition offer: $20 off Premier Membership for new members who sign up May 1–31. Premier is the unlimited tier. This is a meaningful discount on their highest-commitment tier, likely aimed at capturing members before summer competition heats up.

*Source confidence: Season of Strength structure confirmed via OTFInsider.com and OrangeTheory's official promotion page. TRUBAR partnership confirmed via PR Newswire.*

## Notable Patterns

- **Strength is the new cardio narrative.** OrangeTheory (Season of Strength), Barry's (LIFT modality), and Solidcore (always-strength) are all explicitly signaling to consumers that strength training — not cardio metrics — is the premium fitness category. F45 maintains functional training language but is adjacent to this shift. This is a coordinated market signal, not coincidence.

- **Lifestyle ecosystem expansion.** Three of the four are building brand extensions beyond class time: Solidcore's Core Collective wellness partnerships, Barry's Fuel Bar in new studios, OTF's TRUBAR sponsorship. The boutique fitness competitive frame is shifting from "best class" to "best wellness membership."

- **Community and anti-intimidation as acquisition hook.** F45 (no mirrors), OTF (real people campaign), Barry's (connection-first CEO language) — all are framing inclusion and community as the reason to join, not workout intensity. This is a response to the same consumer signal.
